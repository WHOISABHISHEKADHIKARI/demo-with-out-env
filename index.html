About the Hotel

A retreat woven from the hills themselves

Nestled on a quiet ridge above Hetauda, Himalayan Vista has welcomed travellers for over two decades — a place where terraced gardens, warm brick fireplaces and panoramic mountain light replace the noise of the valley floor.

Every suite is framed by the ridges of the Mahabharat range, and every meal is served from our own kitchen garden.

  • Panoramic mountain views from every room
  • Farm-to-table restaurant & in-house bakery
  • Guided treks, heritage walks & conference retreats
Rabin Shrestha Managing Director
